DANIELLE NICOLE MAGUIRE DESIGN

If you want a quote on a project or have any questions please feel free to reach out here:

Hi! My name Danielle Maguire, a Minneapolis-based designer dedicated to creating functional, elevated, and visually compelling spaces for both commercial and residential environments. With a strong focus on form and function, I work to transform spaces into thoughtfully designed experiences that balance beauty, usability, and lasting impact.

My design style leans moody and elegant, layered with rich textures, tonal palettes, and thoughtful patterns. I believe spaces should feel timeless, functional, and inviting—but never too precious. I want your home (or business!) to be a place where people actually live: where friends gather, wine is poured, popcorn is shared, and even the smallest spaces are designed to bring people together.

MOODS

THE MODERN VILLA

This mood board feels warm, sunlit, and richly layered, like golden hour in a Mediterranean villa. The veined marble, deep green tile, natural wood, and soft camel tones create a space that feels grounded yet luminous. It's organic, elevated, and quietly radiant. Poetic but Polished. Light moves across veined marble and warm wood, softened by muted greens and brushed brass. Structured yet organic, bold yet comforting. A layered palette designed to feel intentional, luminous, and deeply grounded.

MODERN HEIRLOOM

This mood board feels moody, tailored, and quietly luxurious, like a modern townhouse with old-soul bones. The black marble, warm wood, brass accents, and classic checker tile give it that elevated European feel, while the clean white cabinetry keeps it grounded and timeless. Confident & Sophisticated. Moody neutrals. Clean lines. Warm metals. A study in contrast where heritage details meet modern clarity. Elevated yet inviting, dramatic yet disciplined, a palette that embodies quiet luxury with presence and poise.

MODERN MOSAIC

This mood board feels artful, grounded, and globally inspired, like a collected home layered over time. The deep blue cabinetry, terracotta tile, terrazzo, patterned motif, and warm copper tones create a palette that feels expressive yet anchored. It's bold, but thoughtful. Vibrant, yet balanced. Elevated & Editorial. Rich terracotta, deep indigo, and expressive pattern layered with intention. A balance of structure and artistry, where global influence meets modern clarity. Grounded yet vibrant, bold yet composed, a palette designed to feel curated, soulful, and enduring.

VERDANT CALM

This mood board feels grounded, elevated, and quietly luxurious, like nature brought indoors but refined through clean, modern restraint. A serene balance of organic texture and modern simplicity where deep mossy greens, warm wood tones, aged metal, and soft stone meet to create a space that feels both grounded and quietly elevated. This palette brings the calm of nature indoors, softened by clean lines and understated warmth for a timeless, restorative atmosphere.
